## Buying Tips for Budget Earbuds

### 1. Check the Return Policy
Budget earbuds can be hit or miss. Always buy from platforms with easy returns.

### 2. Prioritize Your Needs
- **For music**: Look for larger drivers (13mm+)
- **For calls**: Check reviews for mic quality
- **For gaming**: Look for low latency mode
- **For workouts**: IPX4+ water resistance is essential

### 3. Don't Expect Premium Features
ANC, app support, and premium codecs are rare at this price. Focus on sound and battery.

### 4. Check for Warranty
Most budget earbuds come with 6-12 months warranty. Register your product!

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are earbuds under ₹500 worth buying?
Yes! While they won't match premium options, budget earbuds have improved significantly and offer good value for casual users.

### Can I use these for gaming?
Look for models with low latency mode (like pTron Bassbuds Omega) for gaming. Standard latency might cause audio delay.

### How long do budget earbuds last?
With proper care, budget earbuds can last 1-2 years. Battery degradation is the main concern.

### Do these work with both Android and iPhone?
Yes, all Bluetooth earbuds work with any device that supports Bluetooth audio.
