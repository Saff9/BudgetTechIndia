## FAQ

### How many times will a 20000mAh power bank charge my phone?

A 20000mAh power bank will typically charge an average smartphone (4000mAh battery) about 4-5 times, accounting for efficiency losses.

### Is it safe to use a power bank while charging?

While most modern power banks support pass-through charging, it's generally not recommended as it can generate excess heat and reduce battery lifespan.

### Do power banks lose capacity over time?

Yes, power banks gradually lose capacity over time. A quality power bank should retain 80% capacity after 500 charge cycles.

### Can I carry a power bank on a flight?

Yes, power banks under 100Wh (approximately 27000mAh) can be carried in cabin baggage. Check airline regulations for larger capacity units.
