## What to Look for When Buying Budget Earbuds

### Sound Quality

Don't compromise on sound quality just because you're on a budget. Look for:
- Driver size (8mm or larger is good)
- Frequency response range
- Audio codec support (AAC is ideal)

### Battery Life

For daily commuters, battery life is crucial:
- Minimum 4-5 hours per charge
- 20+ hours with case
- Fast charging support

### Comfort

You'll be wearing these for hours:
- Lightweight design (under 5g per earbud)
- Multiple ear tip sizes
- Ergonomic shape

### Durability

Budget doesn't mean disposable:
- IPX4 or higher water resistance
- Quality charging case
- Strong cable connections (for neckbands)
