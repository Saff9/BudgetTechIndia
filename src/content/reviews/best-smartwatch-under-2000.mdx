## Buying Guide: What to Look for in a Budget Smartwatch

### Display Quality
Look for a display with at least 320x385 resolution and 400+ nits brightness for good outdoor visibility.

### Battery Life
Most budget smartwatches offer 5-7 days of battery life. Consider your usage patterns when choosing.

### Health Features
Essential health features to look for:
- Heart rate monitoring
- SpO2 (blood oxygen) tracking
- Sleep tracking
- Step counting

### Water Resistance
IP67 or IP68 rating ensures your watch can handle sweat, rain, and even swimming.

### Sports Modes
If you're into fitness, look for watches with dedicated sports modes for your activities.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I make calls from these smartwatches?
Most budget smartwatches under ₹2000 don't support calling features. You'll need to spend around ₹3000+ for Bluetooth calling functionality.

### Are budget smartwatches accurate for health tracking?
While not as accurate as medical devices or premium smartwatches, budget options provide reasonably good estimates for general fitness tracking.

### Do these watches work with both Android and iPhone?
Yes, all the smartwatches mentioned in this guide are compatible with both Android and iOS devices.
